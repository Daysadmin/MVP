Who we are

Built with
intention.

Days LLC is a small, focused team building tools that help people live well.

Our Mission

Days was born from a simple idea: healing shouldn't require a therapist's waiting room or a bloated app. Small, consistent moments of reflection can change the trajectory of a life.

Why We Built This

We've all had days where we felt lost, stuck, or disconnected. Days is the tool we wished existed — calm, private, and focused purely on helping you feel a little better than yesterday.

How We Operate

Early-stage and building steadily. Our goal is clarity, not chaos — simplicity, not speed for its own sake. Zero interest in surveillance capitalism or bloated bureaucracy.

Where We're Going

Days will continue to evolve based on community feedback. If you have thoughts, we genuinely want to hear them. Reach out anytime — we read everything.

Days LLC — Last updated: February 2026

Days is designed to protect your information and enable you to choose what to share. We collect only what is needed to support your wellness journey and never sell your information.

What We Collect

Account Information: Name (optional), email address, birthday, and location (city/region for timezone-based reminders).

Wellness Activity: Journal entries, meditation usage, mood check-ins, and subscription status.

Device and Usage Data: App version, device type, OS, crash logs, and anonymous analytics (with consent).

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to personalize wellness content, deliver reminders, improve performance, provide support, and offer subscription features. We do not profile you for advertising or sell your data.

Storage and Security

Your data is stored using secure, encrypted infrastructure. Journals are accessible only to you and your authorized devices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share information with trusted infrastructure partners, when legally required, to prevent harm, or with your explicit consent.

Managing Your Data

You can view, update, or delete your account at any time. To delete your data, email support@days.care.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent. Contact privacy@days.care with concerns.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. Contact privacy@days.care.
